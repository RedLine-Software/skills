# Book Mockup Generator

Generate realistic, high-fidelity mockups of paperback pocket books placed on natural wooden surfaces, translating 2D cover artwork into convincing 3D product visuals while strictly preserving the integrity of the original artwork.

## Overview
This skill guides the process of taking a 2D book cover image provided by a user and transforming it into an aesthetically pleasing, photorealistic mockup of a pocket paperback book lying on a natural wooden table in either a top-down or angled perspective. The final output mockup image is strictly formatted in a 1:1 square aspect ratio, and the user-provided cover artwork must never be resized, cropped, or zoomed.

---

## Expected Input
- **Book Cover Image**: A user-uploaded image or asset containing the front cover (and optionally spine/back cover) artwork.
- **Perspective Preference (Optional)**: Top-down (flat lay) or angled (perspective view, e.g., 30°–45° angle showing depth and spine/page edges).
- **Stylistic Elements (Optional)**: Specific preferences for table texture (e.g., light oak, rustic walnut, weathered pine) or complementary decorative props (e.g., coffee cup, glasses, bookmark, plant leaf).

## Expected Output
- A detailed prompt formulation and visual presentation for a realistic mockup featuring:
  - **Aspect Ratio**: Exactly 1:1 square aspect ratio for the overall mockup image.
  - **Artwork Integrity**: Complete, uncompromised front cover artwork with zero cropping, no resizing/scaling distortion, and no zooming in or out.
  - Standard paperback pocket book format (typically 4.25" x 6.87" or standard mass-market/pocket dimensions) adapted to naturally fit the full original cover dimensions.
  - Realistic material qualities: matte or gloss paper texture, natural spine curvature, page ridge texture.
  - Warm, natural lighting and soft shadows onto a detailed wooden table.
  - Accurate cover art projection with zero text distortion, color preservation, and sharp typography.

## Required Data & Tools
- **Cover Asset**: Provided directly by the user.
- **Tools**: Image generation / multimodal visualization tools when available in the environment.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Analyze Input
1. **Dimension & Aspect Ratio Verification**:
   - Inspect the uploaded cover image.
   - Ensure the original proportions, full boundaries, and margins of the cover are identified so that no part of the image is cropped, zoomed, or resized during composition.
2. **Visual Hierarchy & Typography**: Identify main title placement, subtitle, author name, and critical focal elements so they remain uncompromised.
3. **Color Palette & Tone**: Catalog primary, secondary, and accent colors to ensure surrounding elements (lighting, props, wood tone) complement rather than clash.
4. **Finish & Material**: Determine if the cover art suggests a matte, satin, or high-gloss finish.

### Step 2: Determine Perspective and Setting
1. **Canvas Configuration**:
   - Set the output frame to a **1:1 square aspect ratio**.
   - Position the book and composition naturally within the square canvas so the entire cover remains visible without clipping.
2. **Perspective Selection**:
   - **Top-Down (Flat Lay)**: Direct 90° overhead shot. Ideal for minimalist, clean presentations emphasizing the full flat geometry of the cover.
   - **Angled 3/4 View**: Camera positioned at 30°–45° to reveal the spine thickness, paperback page edging, and subtle binding curl.
3. **Surface & Background**:
   - Natural wooden tabletop surface (e.g., rich grain oak, walnut, or raw pine slats).
   - Ensure wood grain direction runs harmoniously (diagonally or parallel) relative to book orientation.
4. **Lighting & Shadows**:
   - Soft, diffused daylight from an off-camera window or gentle studio rim light.
   - Natural contact shadows (ambient occlusion) grounding the book on the wood.
5. **Props (Optional / Subtle)**:
   - Include minimal, non-distracting props if requested or appropriate: ceramic mug, reading glasses, brass pen, or a green leaf cutting to add depth and scale.

### Step 3: Generate Visual Representation
1. **Geometry Mapping & Strict Cover Preservation**:
   - Strictly preserve the original cover image without any cropping, resizing, or zooming.
   - Accurately map the 100% complete 2D cover artwork onto the book surface without stretching, squishing, cutting off edges, or blowing up the image.
   - In angled views, apply authentic physical perspective and foreshortening while ensuring every element of the original cover remains fully present and legible.
2. **Paperback Realism**:
   - Add subtle corner radius wear, gentle spine creasing, and visible paper block thickness with layered page textures.
3. **Fidelity Verification**:
   - Confirm that the final mockup canvas is precisely 1:1 square.
   - Verify that 100% of the original cover is visible with no cutoffs, zooms, or aspect ratio distortions.
   - Verify contrast and readability of all textual elements.
   - Ensure lighting reflections do not obscure primary title or author information.
